Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Refund Policy explains how refunds are handled on Clash Market Global (CMG). By using our platform, you agree to the terms outlined below.

Escrow-Based Transactions

CMG operates as a neutral escrow (middleman) service. Payments made by buyers are temporarily held until the transaction is completed or cancelled according to agreed conditions.

When a Refund Is Issued

A refund may be issued under the following circumstances:

• The seller fails to deliver the agreed digital account or service.
• The transaction is cancelled by mutual agreement of both buyer and seller.
• CMG determines that the transaction cannot be completed due to verified issues.

When Refunds Are Not Available

Refunds will not be provided in the following cases:

• The buyer confirms receipt and satisfaction with the account or service.
• Issues occur after funds have been released to the seller.
• Losses caused by third-party actions, policy violations, or account bans.
• Buyer error, negligence, or change of mind after transaction completion.

Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method. Processing time may vary depending on the payment provider and network conditions.

User Responsibility

Users are responsible for carefully reviewing transaction details before confirming completion. Once funds are released, CMG cannot reverse the transaction.

Final Decision

In disputed cases, CMG reserves the right to make a final decision based on available evidence, communication logs, and platform rules.

By using Clash Market Global (CMG), you acknowledge and agree to this Refund Policy in full.
